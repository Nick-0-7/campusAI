# APEX INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY & SCIENCE
## INSTITUTIONAL EXAMINATION REGULATIONS & ACADEMIC CODE

Section 1: Registration and Course Enrollment
All students must complete their semester course registration within the first five business days of the semester commencement. Course add and drop requests are accepted until the end of the second academic week.

Section 4: Grading Scale & Credit Calculations
The evaluation system is based on a 10-point Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) system. Grades awarded range from O (Outstanding, 10 points) to F (Fail, 0 points). A minimum grade point of 5.0 in each registered course is required to earn course credits.

Section 12: Attendance Requirements
Clause 12.1 - Minimum Semester Attendance:
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in lectures, tutorials, and practical laboratory sessions to be eligible for the semester examination.

Clause 12.2 - Condonation:
Students having attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for medical condonation subject to the submission of valid hospital records endorsed by the Chief Medical Officer and verified by the Dean of Academic Affairs. Students with attendance below 65% are strictly detained and must repeat the course during the supplementary semester.

Section 15: Examination Hall Regulations
Possession of programmable calculators, smart devices, and smartwatches inside the examination hall is strictly prohibited. Any violation is categorized as Level-2 Malpractice resulting in semester debarment.
