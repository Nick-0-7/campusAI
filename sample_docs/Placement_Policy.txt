# CAMPUS PLACEMENT AND CAREER SERVICES POLICY

Section 1: One-Student One-Job Policy
A student who secures an offer with a compensation package above INR 8 LPA is deemed placed and is not eligible to participate in further on-campus drives, except for Dream or Super-Dream companies offering packages exceeding INR 18 LPA.

Section 3: Minimum Placement Criteria
To register with the Career Services Cell, students must possess an overall CGPA of 6.5 or above without active disciplinary holds. A minimum of 80% attendance in Pre-Placement Training (PPT) sessions is mandatory for drive eligibility.
