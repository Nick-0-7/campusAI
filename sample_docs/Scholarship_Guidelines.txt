# CAMPUS MERIT & NEED-BASED SCHOLARSHIPS MANUAL

Section 2: Merit Scholarship Eligibility
Students who maintain a CGPA of 9.0 or higher with zero backlogs in the preceding academic year are eligible for a 50% tuition fee waiver. Top 1% rank holders in each branch receive an additional President's Merit Fellowship grant of INR 25,000 per semester.

Section 5: Means-cum-Merit Financial Aid
Students whose family annual income is below INR 3,00,000 and who maintain at least 7.5 CGPA with at least 80% attendance are eligible for full hostel fee reimbursement. Application deadline is September 15 annually.
